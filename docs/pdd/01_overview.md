# Overview

## Project Name

Intersul

## What is Intersul?

Intersul is a comprehensive service management platform designed specifically for businesses that provide copy machine services. The platform enables service providers to manage their entire operation—from client relationships and service delivery to inventory tracking and workflow automation—through a unified web-based dashboard.

## The Problem It Solves

Service providers in the copy machine industry face significant challenges in managing their operations:

- **Fragmented Operations**: Service activities (maintenance, rentals, sales, and supplies) are often tracked using disparate systems or manual processes, making it difficult to maintain a unified view of business operations.

- **Workflow Complexity**: Service delivery involves multiple steps with different employees, making it challenging to track progress, assign responsibilities, and ensure timely completion.

- **Client Relationship Management**: Maintaining accurate client information, service history, and communication records across multiple touchpoints is time-consuming and error-prone.

- **Inventory Visibility**: Tracking copy machine inventory, supplies, and parts across different service types requires manual coordination and often leads to discrepancies.

- **Onboarding Friction**: Adding new team members to the system and granting appropriate access levels involves manual setup and coordination.

## How It Helps Users

Intersul addresses these challenges by providing:

- **Unified Service Management**: A single platform to manage all service types (maintenance, rentals, sales, and supplies) with consistent workflows and tracking.

- **Automated Workflow Tracking**: Step-based service workflows that automatically assign tasks to employees and track progress from initiation to completion.

- **Centralized Client Management**: Comprehensive client profiles with complete service history, enabling better relationship management and informed decision-making.

- **Real-time Inventory Tracking**: Visibility into copy machine inventory and supplies across all service operations, reducing discrepancies and improving resource allocation.

- **Streamlined Onboarding**: Invitation-based user onboarding that simplifies team member addition and ensures appropriate access control from day one.

- **Data-Driven Insights**: Reporting and analytics capabilities that help identify trends, optimize operations, and make informed business decisions.

## High-Level Goals

1. **Operational Efficiency**: Reduce administrative overhead and manual coordination by automating service workflows and centralizing information.

2. **Service Quality**: Improve service delivery consistency and timeliness through structured workflows and clear accountability.

3. **Client Satisfaction**: Enhance client relationships by providing better service tracking, communication, and responsiveness.

4. **Scalability**: Enable service providers to grow their operations without proportional increases in administrative complexity.

5. **Data Accessibility**: Provide stakeholders with timely access to relevant information for decision-making and reporting.

## Non-Goals

Intersul explicitly does NOT aim to solve:

- **Financial Accounting**: The platform does not include general ledger, accounts payable, or comprehensive financial reporting. It focuses on service operations, not full financial management.

- **Point-of-Sale (POS) Integration**: While the platform tracks sales transactions, it does not replace or integrate with retail POS systems for in-store purchases.

- **Hardware Management**: The platform does not include device-level monitoring, remote diagnostics, or direct machine-to-platform communication. It manages service records, not machine operations.

- **Marketing Automation**: Client relationship management focuses on service history and operational data, not marketing campaigns, email automation, or lead generation.

- **Third-Party Integrations**: The platform does not provide out-of-the-box integrations with external accounting software, CRM systems, or other business tools beyond its core service management scope.

- **Mobile Applications**: The platform is web-based and does not include native mobile applications, though it is designed to be responsive for mobile browser access.
