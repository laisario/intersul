# Functional Overview

## Core Functionalities

Intersul provides a comprehensive set of capabilities designed to support service providers in managing their copy machine service operations. The platform is organized around key functional areas that work together to create a unified service management experience.

## Major System Capabilities

### Service Management

The platform enables users to create, track, and manage services across multiple service types. Services follow structured workflows with defined steps, allowing teams to maintain consistency and accountability throughout the service delivery process. Users can view service details, track progress, assign employees to steps, and maintain complete service history.

### Client Management

Users can maintain comprehensive client profiles containing contact information, service relationships, and complete service history. This capability enables service providers to understand client needs, track interactions, and build stronger relationships through better visibility into past services and patterns.

### Inventory Management

The system tracks copy machine inventory and supplies, providing visibility into available resources across different service types. Users can view machine availability, track supply levels, and understand resource allocation for rentals, sales, and maintenance operations.

### Workflow Automation

Services follow predefined workflows with multiple steps. The system automatically manages step progression, employee assignments, and status tracking. This automation reduces manual coordination and ensures that services progress through all required stages.

### User and Access Management

The platform supports invitation-based user onboarding, allowing administrators to add team members through secure email invitations. Users can be assigned different roles and permissions, ensuring appropriate access control across the organization.

### Reporting and Analytics

Users can access dashboards and reports that provide insights into service operations, client activity, and business performance. This capability supports data-driven decision-making and operational optimization.

## What Users Can Do

### Service Operations

- Create new services for maintenance, rentals, sales, or supplies
- View and manage active services across all types
- Track service progress through workflow steps
- Assign employees to specific service steps
- Update step status and add documentation (images, notes)
- Complete services and maintain service history
- View detailed service information and related data

### Client Management

- Create and maintain client profiles
- View complete service history for each client
- Access client contact information and relationship data
- Track client interactions and service patterns
- Manage client addresses and billing information

### Inventory Operations

- View available copy machines for rental or sale
- Track machine assignments and status
- Monitor supply inventory levels
- Understand resource availability across service types
- View machine details and specifications

### User Administration

- Invite new users via email
- Manage user accounts and permissions
- View user activity and assignments
- Control access to different system features

### Reporting and Analysis

- Access operational dashboards
- View service statistics and trends
- Analyze client activity patterns
- Generate reports on service performance
- Monitor workflow efficiency and bottlenecks

## Functional Scope

### In Scope

The following functional areas are within the platform's scope:

- **Service Lifecycle Management**: Complete management of services from creation through completion, including all workflow steps and status tracking.

- **Client Relationship Management**: Comprehensive client profiles, service history, and relationship data management.

- **Workflow Automation**: Step-based workflows with employee assignment, status tracking, and progress management.

- **Inventory Tracking**: Copy machine and supply inventory visibility and tracking across service types.

- **User Management**: Invitation-based onboarding, authentication, and role-based access control.

- **Operational Reporting**: Dashboards, statistics, and reports focused on service operations and client activity.

- **Multi-Service Type Support**: Unified management of maintenance, rental, sale, and supplies services.

- **Documentation Management**: Ability to attach images and notes to service steps for documentation and audit purposes.

### Out of Scope

The following functional areas are explicitly outside the platform's scope:

- **Financial Accounting**: General ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and comprehensive financial reporting are not included.

- **Point-of-Sale Systems**: The platform does not replace retail POS systems or handle in-store transaction processing.

- **Hardware Monitoring**: Direct machine-to-platform communication, remote diagnostics, and real-time device monitoring are not supported.

- **Marketing Automation**: Email campaigns, lead generation, marketing analytics, and customer acquisition tools are not included.

- **Third-Party Integrations**: Out-of-the-box integrations with external accounting software, CRM platforms, or other business systems are not provided.

- **Mobile Native Applications**: Native iOS or Android applications are not part of the platform, though web-based mobile access is supported.

- **Advanced Analytics**: Predictive analytics, machine learning, or advanced business intelligence beyond operational reporting are not included.

- **Document Management**: Comprehensive document storage, versioning, or document workflow management beyond service step documentation is not supported.
