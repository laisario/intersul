# Personas

This document describes the primary user personas for the Intersul platform. Each persona represents a distinct user type with specific needs, goals, and pain points that the platform addresses.

## Persona 1: Service Manager

### Name
Maria Silva

### Role
Service Manager / Operations Coordinator

### Background
Maria has been working in the copy machine service industry for 8 years. She oversees a team of 12 field technicians and coordinates service delivery across maintenance, rentals, and sales. She spends most of her day managing service schedules, assigning technicians to jobs, tracking service progress, and ensuring timely completion. Maria uses spreadsheets, phone calls, and a whiteboard to coordinate operations, which becomes increasingly challenging as the business grows.

### Goals
- Ensure all services are completed on time and meet quality standards
- Optimize technician assignments to maximize efficiency
- Maintain visibility into service progress across all active jobs
- Identify bottlenecks and workflow issues quickly
- Provide accurate service status updates to clients when requested
- Generate reports for management on service performance and team productivity

### Pain Points
- **Lack of Real-Time Visibility**: Maria struggles to know the current status of services without making multiple phone calls or checking different systems.
- **Manual Coordination Overhead**: Assigning technicians, tracking progress, and updating statuses requires constant manual intervention and communication.
- **Information Fragmentation**: Service information is scattered across spreadsheets, notes, and memory, making it difficult to get a complete picture.
- **Difficulty Tracking Workflows**: Complex services with multiple steps are hard to track, leading to missed steps or delays.
- **Reporting Challenges**: Compiling service statistics and performance reports requires manual data collection and analysis.

### How the Product Helps Them
Intersul provides Maria with a unified dashboard where she can see all active services at a glance. The workflow automation ensures that services progress through defined steps, and she can easily assign technicians to steps with clear accountability. Real-time status tracking eliminates the need for constant phone calls, and the reporting capabilities give her immediate access to performance metrics. The platform's structured approach reduces coordination overhead and helps her identify issues before they become problems.

## Persona 2: Field Technician

### Name
Carlos Mendes

### Role
Field Service Technician

### Background
Carlos is an experienced field technician with 5 years of experience in copy machine maintenance and repair. He typically handles 6-8 service calls per day, traveling between client locations. Carlos needs to know his daily schedule, access service details and client information, update service progress, and document his work. He primarily uses his smartphone for communication and information access while in the field.

### Goals
- Know his daily schedule and service assignments clearly
- Access service details and client information quickly while on-site
- Document completed work efficiently (photos, notes, status updates)
- Understand what steps are required for each service
- Track his own productivity and completed services
- Communicate service status back to the office without delays

### Pain Points
- **Unclear Assignments**: Carlos sometimes receives unclear or incomplete information about service requirements, leading to return visits.
- **Difficulty Updating Status**: Updating service progress requires phone calls or waiting until he returns to the office, causing delays in information flow.
- **Documentation Burden**: Taking notes and photos on paper or personal devices creates organization challenges and potential loss of information.
- **Limited Context**: He often lacks access to service history or client preferences while on-site, making it harder to provide personalized service.
- **Schedule Changes**: Last-minute schedule changes or reassignments are communicated inconsistently, causing confusion.

### How the Product Helps Them
Intersul gives Carlos clear visibility into his assigned service steps through the platform's mobile-responsive interface. He can access complete service details, client information, and service history directly from his smartphone while on-site. The step-based workflow clarifies exactly what needs to be done, and he can update step status and add documentation (photos, notes) immediately, keeping information current. The platform ensures he receives assignments clearly and can track his progress throughout the day.

## Persona 3: Business Owner / Administrator

### Name
Roberto Alves

### Role
Business Owner / Administrator

### Background
Roberto owns and operates a copy machine service business with 25 employees across three service areas: maintenance, rentals, and sales. He has been in business for 15 years and is focused on growth and operational efficiency. Roberto needs to understand business performance, make strategic decisions, manage client relationships, and ensure the organization operates smoothly. He spends time on high-level oversight, client relationship management, and strategic planning.

### Goals
- Understand overall business performance and identify growth opportunities
- Maintain strong client relationships and ensure client satisfaction
- Make data-driven decisions about operations, staffing, and resource allocation
- Ensure operational efficiency and cost control
- Scale the business without proportional increases in administrative complexity
- Have confidence that services are being delivered consistently and professionally

### Pain Points
- **Limited Business Visibility**: Roberto struggles to get a clear picture of business performance without spending significant time on data collection and analysis.
- **Client Relationship Gaps**: He lacks easy access to complete client service history, making it difficult to have informed conversations and identify opportunities.
- **Scaling Challenges**: As the business grows, manual processes become bottlenecks, and adding new team members requires significant administrative overhead.
- **Inconsistent Service Delivery**: Without standardized workflows, service quality and processes vary, making it hard to ensure consistency.
- **Resource Allocation Uncertainty**: He has difficulty understanding resource utilization and availability, making it challenging to optimize operations.

### How the Product Helps Them
Intersul provides Roberto with comprehensive dashboards and reporting that give him immediate insights into business performance, service trends, and operational metrics. The platform's client management capabilities ensure he has complete visibility into client relationships and service history, enabling better client conversations and relationship management. The structured workflows ensure consistent service delivery, and the invitation-based onboarding simplifies team expansion. The platform's automation reduces administrative overhead, allowing the business to scale more efficiently while maintaining operational control and visibility.

## Persona 4: Office Administrator

### Name
Ana Costa

### Role
Office Administrator / Client Service Coordinator

### Background
Ana handles client communications, service scheduling, data entry, and administrative tasks for the service provider. She is often the first point of contact for clients and coordinates between clients, field technicians, and management. Ana manages client information, processes service requests, handles phone calls, and maintains records. She works primarily from the office but needs to access information quickly to respond to client inquiries.

### Goals
- Respond to client inquiries quickly and accurately
- Schedule services efficiently and avoid conflicts
- Maintain accurate and up-to-date client information
- Process new service requests smoothly
- Provide clients with service status updates when requested
- Support field technicians with information and coordination

### Pain Points
- **Information Access Delays**: Finding client information or service status requires checking multiple sources, slowing response times to clients.
- **Data Entry Duplication**: Client and service information is entered multiple times in different systems, increasing errors and workload.
- **Status Update Challenges**: Getting current service status requires contacting field technicians, causing delays in client communication.
- **Scheduling Complexity**: Coordinating service schedules while avoiding conflicts and considering technician availability is time-consuming.
- **Incomplete Client Context**: She often lacks complete service history when speaking with clients, limiting her ability to provide comprehensive support.

### How the Product Helps Them
Intersul provides Ana with immediate access to client profiles and complete service history, enabling her to respond to client inquiries quickly and accurately. The platform's unified system eliminates data duplication, and real-time service status updates mean she can provide current information to clients without delays. The scheduling and assignment capabilities help her coordinate services more efficiently, and the comprehensive client context ensures she can provide better support during client interactions.
